AIRPORT_AUTHORITY_DATA_ANALYSIS

This study examines important metrics like passenger volume, flight schedules, cargo handling, revenue streams, infrastructure use, and sustainability in order to improve operational efficiency using data from the Airport Authority. The study finds inefficiencies and growth prospects that can enhance the traveler experience and streamline airport operations by utilizing advanced analytics and Business Intelligence (BI) tools. The results demonstrate how data-driven decision-making can simplify airport operations, improve security, and guarantee sustainable growth.

KEYWORDS: Business Intelligence, Airport Data Analysis, Operational Efficiency, Passenger Traffic, Predictive Analytics, Cargo Handling, Revenue Optimization, Sustainability
